No _detail_hero.html here: that component expects a single hero image and a list of tracks to play, and neither exists for a mood — there's no artwork representing "Sad" the way an avatar represents an artist, and nothing to Play all until a specific playlist below is opened. A plain title plus a shelf of playlist cards is the whole panel. Cards reuse .rec-card, the same class Explore's own chart shelves render client-side (see home/explore.js's recPlaylistCardHtml) — same look, and clicking one opens it through the ordinary yt-playlist route (see home/detail.js's click handler). A grid rather than Explore's .shelf-row slider. A slider is right on Explore, where a shelf is one of several and has to give the ones below it room; here the playlists are the entire page, and a single row of them with the other twenty scrolled off the side made a mood look nearly empty. Stacking downward in fours fills the panel with what is actually in it. .shelf-row also carries the drag-to-scroll wiring (see home/scrollers.js), which a grid must not have — hence a class of its own rather than an override. #}
