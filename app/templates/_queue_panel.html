Rows come from _content_row.html — the same template a playlist's track list uses — so a queue row is identical to the row it was queued from, and clicking one plays it without any extra wiring: the href is the ordinary /#player/ route, and queue.js's noteCurrent moves the pointer to whatever opens (see its docstring). The whole queue, including what's already played, and with nothing here saying which row is current — home/overlay.js's markCurrentQueueRow puts the marker on and moves it. That split is the point: the pointer changes every time a track ends or is picked, and re-rendering for it was what made the list rebuild itself under the finger that had just chosen a row. Filled by GET /partials/queue, which is handed the ids by the client — the queue's order lives in the browser, not the database. #}
