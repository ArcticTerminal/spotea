{# Library's "New releases": everything the artists you follow have put out this year. GET /partials/detail/playlist/new-uploads returns this, and Home's shelf of the same name links here — the shelf shows twelve, this shows all of them. A grid rather than a track list, which is what this tile used to open. It held Content rows flagged `is_new_upload`: tracks from releases that appeared *after* the follow, expiring after fourteen days. Measured on a real library it was three tracks with a fortnight to live, and it could never show anything an artist released before you followed them. Releases read off `Artist.release_snapshot` have neither limit and cost no request. So there is no Play all here, and that is deliberate rather than missing: a release carries no video ids until it is opened, so "play all of these" would mean opening every one of them — a live request each. Open a release and it has its own Play all, exactly like an album on an artist's page. A release holding one track skips the panel and plays. .mood-grid rather than a class of its own: a mood's playlists are the same shape of thing on the same kind of page (see _mood_panel.html), and the grid was built there for the same reason — a row that scrolls sideways makes a full page look nearly empty. #}
